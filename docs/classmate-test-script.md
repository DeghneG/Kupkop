# KupKop classmate test (about 10 minutes)

**This is a prototype. Do not call real clinics.** Nothing you do here reaches 911, real rescuers or real clinics. Use a toy, a plush animal, or a printed photo of a dog or cat outdoors, and a **fake or covered** car plate.

## Before you start (the organizer)
- Two phones: **Phone A** (reporter, any new account) and **Phone B** (a volunteer account the admin approved).
- Phone B: Settings → Alerts → **Turn on alerts**, then **Use where I am now** with 3 km.
- One clinic staff account for "QA Clinic" (optional, for steps 8–9).

## Scenario
You are walking home and find an injured dog by the road. Report it, get it to a clinic, and follow its recovery.

## Steps
1. **Phone A:** open the site and tap the red **SOS** button. If asked, allow the camera and location.
2. Point at the "injured dog", pick **Dog** and **Hit & Run**, and tap the shutter.
3. Check the street name and add a note ("by the red gate"), then tap **Dispatch Emergency Report**. Sign in if asked.
4. **Phone B:** within a minute, an alert should arrive, even with the browser closed. Tap it.
5. **Phone B:** tap **Claim Rescue**. Check the bar at the bottom shows the 60-minute lock.
6. **Phone A:** open the case in the feed. It should say who is on the way.
7. **Phone B:** tap **Admit**, choose a clinic, and confirm.
8. **Clinic staff:** open Settings → **Clinic care**, find the case under **Waiting for intake**, and tap **Animal arrived**.
9. **Clinic staff:** tap **Post recovery update**, pick **Healing**, and write a short note.
10. **Phone A:** open the case again. The recovery note should be there.

**Extra (if time):** on Phone B, open a new case, tap **Hand off to someone else**, and photograph a fake plate.

## Watch for
- Any screen where you didn't know what to tap next, or had to ask.
- Anything cut off, hard to read in sunlight, or too small to tap.
- Anything that took longer than expected (write the step and the seconds).
- Any message that seemed wrong or untrue.

## Three questions at the end
1. At which step did you hesitate the most, and why?
2. If this were a real injured dog, would you trust KupKop to get it help? What would make you trust it more?
3. What one thing would you change first?
