# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
- **Primary:** Panicked bystanders or street passersby holding or witnessing an injured street animal (aspin / puspin) outdoors under stress, rain, or harsh tropical sun with one hand.
- **Responders:** Vetted community volunteers claiming rescues to prevent duplicate efforts.
- **Clinics:** Listed partner veterinary clinics receiving injured animals under 14-day activity clocks.
- **Admin:** Network verifiers reviewing responder applications and emergency escalations.

## Product Purpose
Fast, community-driven street animal emergency dispatch, claim locking, and recovery tracking. Eliminates bystander panic with live-camera GPS reporting, bilingual first-aid guidance from Rafa AI, and 14-day facility inactivity tracking.

## Positioning
Philippines-first street animal emergency network enforcing unforgeable live-camera evidence (no gallery uploads), tamper-proof atomic server timestamps, 15-second 3-tap dispatch, and calm bilingual stabilization advice.
